JAPANESE PORK:A CULINARY TREASURE

Japanese cuisine is celebrated across the world for its exceptional quality, meticulous craftsmanship and pure, natural flavours. No visit to Japan, whether for business or leisure, is complete without savouring the freshness and excellence of its ingredients.

Among these, Japanese pork is truly remarkable. Renowned for its perfect balance of umami, tenderness, juiciness and subtle sweetness, it has become a sought-after choice for discerning food lovers and chefs alike.

Each region of Japan brings its own heritage and precision to pork production. From carefully curated feed to time-honoured breeding practices, every cut reflects the care and expertise that define Japanese culinary excellence.

Experience the extraordinary at the Oishii J-Pork Festival. Explore premium cuts, top J-Pork brands and a celebration of Japan’s finest pork, crafted to delight the most refined palates.

A Symphony of Tenderness and Taste

Whether thinly sliced for shabu-shabu or gently grilled for yakiniku, Japanese pork reveals an exquisite balance of succulence and flavour. Its smooth texture, gentle juiciness, and refined marbling create a melt-in-the-mouth experience that delights even the most discerning palate, an orchestration of taste and texture in perfect harmony.

Excellence at Every Stage

Behind every cut of Japanese pork lies a deep commitment to quality. From carefully maintained farms to state-of-the-art processing facilities, each step adheres to rigorous safety and hygiene standards, including HACCP certification. Every piece is fully traceable from farm to table, ensuring confidence, transparency, and excellence in every serving.

Nourished with Care, Perfected in Taste

The superior flavour of Japanese pork begins with the animals’ well-being. Raised on balanced, nutrient-rich feed made from high-quality grains, rice, and sustainable food resources, the pigs thrive in an environment designed for comfort and health. This mindful approach results in pork that is not only tender and flavourful but also a reflection of Japan’s dedication to harmony between nature and nourishment.

A Journey Through Japan’s Finest Cuts

Across Japan, more than 400 distinct pork brands embody the spirit of their regions, each shaped by local climate, feed, and farming philosophy. From Hokkaido’s pristine pastures to Kyushu’s fertile plains, every variety offers a unique expression of taste and texture. Discover a world of refined flavours, where every dish tells the story of craftsmanship, tradition, and passion.

JAPANESE PORK CREATIONS

Savour the refined flavours of dishes made with Japan’s finest pork

Each of these dishes showcases the remarkable qualities of Japanese pork, from its delicate tenderness to its refined depth of flavour and exceptional quality. Together, they offer a dining experience that lingers on the palate, turning every meal into a sophisticated celebration of taste and culinary artistry.

Tonkatsu

Tonkatsu, a quintessential Japanese classic, features a tender pork cutlet enveloped in a golden, crisp panko crust. Traditionally finished with a subtly sweet and savoury sauce, it is served with freshly shredded cabbage, delicate rice, and a comforting bowl of miso soup. Each bite delivers a refined contrast of textures and flavours.

Butadon

Butadon presents thinly sliced Japanese pork gently simmered in a savoury soy-based broth with tender onions, layered over fragrant, fluffy rice. Each mouthful is a comforting embrace, with the pork’s natural sweetness harmonising with the umami depth of the broth. Known as “tondon,” this dish exemplifies the heartwarming elegance of Japanese cuisine.

Shabu Shabu

Shabu shabu is a luxurious, interactive feast. Ultra-thin slices of Japanese pork and crisp seasonal vegetables are briefly swished through a fragrant, bubbling broth, preserving the pork’s natural tenderness and subtle sweetness. Each swish delivers a symphony of aroma, flavour, and texture.

Shogayaki

Thinly sliced pork loin is expertly pan-fried and infused with soy, mirin, sake, and ginger, creating a sophisticated balance of sweetness, umami, and gentle spice.

Kakuni

Tender pork belly is slow-cooked in a rich, soy-based broth until melt-in-the-mouth, with layers of fat and meat enriched by the savoury-sweet braising liquid.

Pork Sauté

Juicy pork chops or steaks are seared to perfection and complemented by soy-based or mustard sauces, with earthy vegetables enhancing the rich, savoury flavours of premium Japanese pork.
